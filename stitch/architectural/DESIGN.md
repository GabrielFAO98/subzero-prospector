## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the calculated elegance of high-end structural engineering and contemporary architectural mastery. It targets ultra-high-net-worth clients, institutional developers, and award-winning architectural consortiums who demand technical rigor paired with an uncompromising aesthetic vision.

The visual style synthesizes **Architectural Minimalism** with **Refined Dark Tonal Layers**:
- Deep void-like slate foundations evoke permanence, monolithic stone, and structural steel.
- Warm champagne and burnished bronze accents echo brass joinery, architectural models, and luxury metal finishes.
- Spatial precision and structural balance dominate layout compositions, using hairline delineations, deliberate grid geometry, and expansive negative space.
- The UI evokes solemn confidence, intellectual prestige, and uncompromising fidelity.

## Colors

The palette operates under an uncompromising dark mode environment inspired by blueprint ateliers, dark slate quarry stone, and polished architectural alloys.

- **Primary (`#C5A880`)**: Muted champagne bronze used for prime focal accents, active state indicators, key architectural metrics, and refined interactive highlights.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37`)**: Warm brushed gold reserved for luxury badges, precision insignia, accolades, and key transactional triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#E2D9CC`)**: Platinum-bone tinted metallic surface layer used for subtle glows, low-key interactive borders, and selected micro-copy.
- **Neutral Slate Foundation (`#0F172A`)**: The primary canvas tone, modulated by deeper spatial wells (`#020617`) for backgrounds and elevated structural slabs (`#1E293B`) for overlays and cards.
- **Text & Readability**: Primary copy sits at `#F8FAFC` for high architectural legibility, while secondary annotations, engineering tags, and structural metadata utilize `#94A3B8`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary authority of **Noto Serif** with the engineered precision of **Space Grotesk**.

- **Editorial Headings (`Noto Serif`)**: Communicates poise, timeless luxury, and cultural weight. Used for hero statements, chapter intros, and project showcase titles. Renders strictly in regular weights (`400`) to evoke classic architectural monographs.
- **Structural Text & Data (`Space Grotesk`)**: Provides technical clarity, mathematical rhythm, and geometric balance. Used for all narrative body text, technical engineering specs, and interactive controls.
- **Case Conventions**: All `label-sm` and `label-md` tokens are rendered in uppercase to mimic architectural blueprint title blocks and CAD annotations.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an asymmetric architectural grid system based on golden ratios and structural module repetition.

- **Desktop (1440px+)**: 12-column modular grid with `gutter-desktop` (2.5rem) and generous framing margins of `margin-desktop` (4rem). Elements may align deliberately off-center to emphasize cantilevered layouts and white space.
- **Tablet (768px - 1439px)**: 8-column layout with `gutter` (1.5rem) and `margin` (2rem). Multi-column technical specifications compress into two-column dossiers.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column layout with `gutter-mobile` (1rem) and `margin-mobile` (1.25rem). Full-bleed image treatments pair with stacked linear metrics.
- **Spatial Rhythm**: Whitespace is treated as a solid structural material. Ample vertical rhythm ensures that project imagery, engineering schematics, and editorial statements never compete for visual prominence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **Tonal Monoliths** and **Subtle Micro-Borders** rather than theatrical drop shadows:

- **Base Layer (`#020617`)**: Deep slate foundation representing infinite architectural space.
- **Intermediate Surface (`#0F172A`)**: Standard viewport background for reading planes and project narratives.
- **Elevated Cantilevers (`#1E293B`)**: Cards, technical drawers, and floating command surfaces. Elevated cards feature 1px structural micro-borders using `rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.15)` (muted champagne hairline).
- **Frosted Translucency**: Sticky navigation bars and floating spatial overlays apply backdrop filters (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) over `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)` to allow imagery to subtly pass underneath without compromising architectural legibility.
- **Ambient Accent Shadows**: Modals and high-priority overlays use directional, ultra-soft tinted diffusion: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(2, 6, 23, 0.85), 0 0 0 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts absolute sharpness (`roundedness: 0`). 

- Every interactive button, image container, dialog, input box, and trust badge adheres to crisp 90-degree corners, reflecting structural steel columns, cut slate, and uncompromising technical precision.
- No rounded border radii are permitted.
- Deliberate 1px border lines serve as tectonic seams between interface modules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Pure sharp rectangle. Background is `#C5A880`, text is `#020617` (Space Grotesk, bold, uppercase tracking). On hover, background shifts seamlessly to `#D4AF37` with an subtle inner hairline border in `#F8FAFC`.
- **Secondary Button (Architectural Blueprint)**: Sharp 1px outline in `rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.4)` over transparent slate. Text in `#F8FAFC`. Hover fills to `rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.08)` with border color shifting to `#C5A880`.
- **Text Action**: Monospaced/geometric label with a trailing dynamic arrow (`→`), underlined with a 1px bronze rule that expands from center on hover.

### Trust Badges & Certifications
- Compact horizontal modules framed with hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(226, 217, 204, 0.2)`).
- Typography uses `label-sm` uppercase tracking with subtle gold accent icons representing structural certifications (e.g., LEED Platinum, CTBUH, Structural Fellow credentials).

### Cards & Project Dossiers
- Monolithic `#1E293B` containers wrapped in a 1px border (`rgba(226, 217, 204, 0.1)`).
- Images feature a desaturated, high-contrast dark architectural treatment that animates smoothly to full warm balance upon hover.
- Metadata headers (square footage, load capacity, seismic rating) are styled using `label-md` in `#C5A880`.

### Chips & Engineering Tags
- Sharp rectangular capsules with `0.25rem` padding vertical, `0.75rem` horizontal.
- Dark slate fill (`#020617`) with a 1px border in `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`. Label text set in `label-sm`, `#94A3B8`.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Flat rectangular field with an explicit 1px bottom border in `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.4)` and a translucent dark background (`rgba(2, 6, 23, 0.4)`).
- Focused state transforms the border into a solid `#C5A880` 1px perimeter box with floating labels transitioning to `#C5A880`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Geometric square indicators (0px radius) with 1px border (`rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.5)`).
- Checked state displays a solid `#C5A880` fill with a sharp `#020617` centered square pip, avoiding rounded iconography.

### Data & Technical Metric Displays
- Custom component displaying engineering metrics: Massive numerals in `Noto Serif` (`display-md`) paired with micro-labels in uppercase `Space Grotesk` (`label-sm`), separated by a razor-thin 1px metallic champagne divider.
